Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about PhisDetect

How does URL scanning work?

When you submit a URL, PhisDetect analyzes it through multiple layers:

  • Domain reputation — Checks if the domain is known for phishing
  • SSL certificate validation — Verifies if the connection is secure
  • Redirect chain analysis — Detects malicious redirects
  • Blacklist checking — Compares against known phishing databases
  • AI-powered risk scoring — Uses machine learning to predict threats

The analysis typically completes within 2–5 seconds.

How does email scanning work?

Our email scanner examines several aspects of suspicious emails:

  • Natural Language Processing — Detects urgent or manipulative language
  • Grammar analysis — Identifies poor grammar common in phishing
  • Link extraction — Finds and analyzes embedded links
  • Sender domain verification — Checks if the sender is legitimate
  • Attachment scanning — Detects suspicious file types
  • Spoofing detection — Identifies impersonation attempts

Simply paste the email content or headers and we'll handle the rest.

How does QR code scanning work?

QR scanning follows a simple process:

  • Image upload — Upload a QR code image or drag and drop
  • Decoding — Extract the URL from the QR code
  • URL analysis — The extracted URL is automatically scanned using our threat detection
  • Risk assessment — Get a detailed threat analysis with confidence scores

This is perfect for verifying QR codes you find in emails, posters, or physical locations.

Is my data private and secure?

Absolutely. We take your privacy seriously:

  • No data storage — URLs and emails are analyzed in real-time and not permanently stored
  • Anonymous scanning — No personal information is required
  • Secure connection — All data is transmitted over HTTPS
  • No third-party sharing — Your scans and reports are kept confidential
  • Regular security audits — We continuously monitor and improve our security

We recommend using PhisDetect as a tool, but always exercise caution online.

How is my data stored?

Data storage follows these principles:

  • Minimal collection — We only store what's necessary for your reports
  • Encrypted database — All stored data is encrypted
  • Anonymous reports — Report submissions are anonymized
  • Data retention — Historical data is retained to track trends
  • GDPR compliant — We follow data protection regulations

You can request data deletion at any time through your account settings.

How do reports work?

When you submit a threat report:

  • Report submission — Click the "Report Threat" button after analysis
  • Automated reporting — Reports are sent to cybersecurity organizations
  • Points reward — You earn +10 points for each report
  • History tracking — All your reports are saved to your dashboard
  • Community protection — Your report helps protect other users

Each report is reviewed and contributes to our threat intelligence database.

How do points work?

Points are our way of rewarding vigilant users:

  • +5 points — Detecting a threat (URL, Email, or QR scan)
  • +10 points — Submitting a threat report
  • Bonus points — Extra points for reporting critical threats
  • Leaderboard — Compete with other users for top ranking
  • Badges — Achieve milestones and earn special recognition

Your points contribute to your rank on the leaderboard.

How do I create an account?

Creating an account is simple:

  • Click Settings — Access the settings option in your profile
  • Sign up — Enter your email and create a password
  • Verify email — Confirm your email address
  • Start scanning — Begin earning points and submitting reports

Coming soon: Google and GitHub OAuth integration!
